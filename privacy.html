Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

TheRoastFeed Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API. It is not distributed to the public.

Data the App accesses

  • Your YouTube channel information (channel ID, title) — to confirm uploads go to the correct channel.
  • Permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload scope).
  • OAuth tokens issued by Google, stored securely on the owner's private server.

How data is used

Data is used solely to authenticate with Google and upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained until the owner revokes access (via Google Account settings) or deletes them. Uploaded video metadata remains on YouTube under the owner's channel.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through the YouTube channel page.
